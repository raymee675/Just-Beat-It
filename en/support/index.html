Frequently asked questions

What do I need to play?

A Windows PC with a 64-bit processor and operating system, and a keyboard. See the system information on the game page and the Steam store for details.

Can I play with a controller?

A keyboard is required. This edition does not list full or partial controller support.

Can I play offline or with other people?

This is a single-player game. You can play offline after installation.

Why did I get hit while dashing?

Dashes started less than 0.40 seconds after the previous dash movement ends do not grant invincibility. Leave more time between dashes; see the manual for details.

How do I change the language or volume?

Press Esc to open settings. Choose Language, Master Volume, Music or Sound Effects. Background contrast and reduced camera motion are also available.

Does it support Steam Cloud, achievements or leaderboards?

This edition does not support Steam Cloud, Steam achievements or Steam leaderboards. Your clears, best scores and fewest hits for each stage are saved on this PC as local clear records under “Achievements” on the title screen.

The game will not start. What should I send?

Check the system information and Steam’s file verification option. If the problem continues, include your Windows version, CPU, GPU, memory, game version, the steps that trigger it and any error message when contacting support. Do not include passwords or payment information.
