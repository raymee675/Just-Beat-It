01 / Keyboard controls

Just Beat It is a single-player game. A keyboard is required.

ActionKeys
Move / selectArrow keys / WASD
DashSpace / Z / Left Shift
Confirm in menusSpace / Z
Pause / settings / backEsc
Fullscreen / windowAlt + Enter

02 / Dash through danger

Move to find an opening, then dash through a dense pattern. A dash grants invincibility when you leave enough time after the previous dash. If you release the movement keys, the dash follows your last movement direction.

Rapid repeated dashes are not invincible.

Starting another dash less than 0.40 seconds after the previous dash movement ends gives movement only, without invincibility. Leave at least 0.40 seconds after that movement ends to regain an invincible dash.

03 / Graze, score & results

Passing close to a bullet earns graze points when that bullet first enters the graze ring. Keep grazing without getting hit to increase the chain multiplier. A hit resets the chain.

Base score
200,000 × song progress − 8,000 per hit (minimum 0).
Graze points
100 × chain multiplier per graze. The multiplier increases by 0.5 every 25 consecutive grazes, up to ×4.
Dash dodges
1,000 points when a bullet overlaps your hit area during an invincible dash.
Total score
(Base + graze + dash dodge points) × difficulty multiplier, rounded and capped at 999,999.

The results screen shows your rank, score, hits, grazes and dash dodges. Review the score breakdown, reduce hits and refine your route for your next run.

04 / Stages & difficulty

Select a stage with Up / Down, a difficulty with Left / Right, then press Space / Z to start. All ten stages offer Easy, Normal and Hard.

DifficultyChallengeScore multiplier

05 / Settings, pause & language

Press Esc on the title screen for settings, or during play to pause. Use Up / Down or W / S to select, Left / Right or A / D to adjust, Space / Z to confirm, and Esc to return.

  • Language: switch between Japanese and English. The initial language follows the OS unless a preference has been saved.
  • Master, music and sound effects volumes can be adjusted separately. Set music or sound effects to 0% to mute that category.
  • Background Contrast: raise the value to darken backgrounds and make the bullets and player easier to see.
  • Reduce Camera Motion: reduces screen shake, automatic camera movement, blur and distortion.
  • From pause settings, choose Resume or Return to Title. Quit Game asks for confirmation: Y to quit; N or Esc to cancel.
